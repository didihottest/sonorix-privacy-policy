Privacy Policy for Sonorix

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Sonorix ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sonorix mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Sonorix is an offline-first ambient sound & audio player application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or sensitive device information. All user preferences, custom mix presets, and sound configurations remain stored strictly on your local device.

2. Permissions Used

Notification Access (Optional): Used solely to detect local media playback state to auto-pause ambient sound or overlay audio. No notification content, metadata, or personal messaging data is logged, collected, or uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services

Sonorix does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

If you have any questions regarding privacy, contact us at: support@ekoudynamics.com
