Privacy Policy for Sonorix

Developer: Ekou Dynamics | Effective Date: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the mobile application Sonorix ("App"), created by Ekou Dynamics ("we", "us", or "our"). Sonorix is designed as an offline-first ambient audio mixer and sleep aid application. We value user privacy and are committed to full transparency regarding data practices and device permission usage in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Data Collection, Storage, and Sharing

Sonorix does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) or sensitive personal user data.

  • Personal Data: We do not ask for, harvest, or track names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, accounts, or location data.
  • Device Data: We do not log unique device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID).
  • Local Storage: All user settings, custom audio mix presets, volume configurations, and preferences are stored exclusively on your local device memory using Android local database/storage APIs. This data never leaves your device.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose Declarations

To deliver core sound mixing features, Sonorix requests limited Android device permissions. Each permission serves a explicit, restricted purpose:

  • Notification Access (Notification Listener Service / BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE):
    Purpose: Optional feature used solely to detect active background audio sessions from third-party media apps (e.g., Spotify, YouTube Music). This allows Sonorix to automatically pause or adjust ambient sound playback when other media starts playing.
    Data Handling: No notification content, text messages, caller details, or notification metadata are recorded, read, stored, or transmitted off the device.
  • Foreground Service & Audio Focus (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK):
    Purpose: Enables uninterrupted audio playback when the App runs in the background or when the screen is locked, displaying standard playback controls in the system notification drawer.
    Data Handling: Controls local audio engine state only. No data collected.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS - Android 13+):
    Purpose: Displays active audio control notifications (Play/Pause/Timer) in the system tray while audio is active.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK):
    Purpose: Prevents the processor from sleeping during scheduled sleep timer sessions so audio fades out smoothly.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Advertising

Sonorix does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking services (such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust, or AppsFlyer), or social media SDKs. No background data telemetry is transmitted to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Sonorix does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). Because the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations regarding minors.

6. Security

Since all application configuration data remains strictly local on the user's Android device, security relies on standard Android OS sandboxing mechanisms protecting app-private storage.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal policies or app functionality. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
